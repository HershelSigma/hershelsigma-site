About Hershel Sigma AI

Hershel Sigma LLC · Updated October 4, 2026

Hershel Sigma AI is the company behind Hershel, an AI dialer for high-volume outbound sales and appointment-setting floors. Hershel Sigma LLC builds it and runs it for each client as a managed service, around the clock. Nathan Yonash founded the company, and Hershel was built on the solar appointment-setting floors he runs in Texas and Florida.

What Hershel is

Hershel is an AI dialer built on a proven open-source dialing engine, then rebuilt around AI: its own rep console, its own manager screens, and AI that runs dial pace, caller IDs and call notes.

Day to day, that means the dialer paces itself. Dialler Manager reads live campaign stats about every 10 seconds and moves the dial level so reps stay on calls while the drop rate stays under the ceiling the floor sets. The Shield watches every outbound caller ID for spam flags and carrier refusals and swaps out the numbers that go bad. Closable writes a brief from each appointment-set call and posts it on the contact in GoHighLevel before the closer shows up.

Each client gets its own dedicated dialer server. For how the pieces work together, read what an AI dialer is, or start at the homepage.

Who's behind it

Nathan Yonash started Hershel Sigma AI while running outbound solar appointment-setting floors in Texas and Florida. Hershel began as the tooling for those floors, something to keep the dial pace right as answer rates moved and to get flagged caller IDs out of rotation before they cost a day of calls. It's now offered to other outbound teams as a managed service. The legal company name is Hershel Sigma LLC.

How to spell Hershel

It's Hershel, H-E-R-S-H-E-L, with no c. The company is Hershel Sigma AI, the product is Hershel, and the site is hershelsigma.ai.

It gets typed a lot of ways: Herschel Sigma, Hershal Sigma, Hershell Sigma, Hersel Sigma. They all point to the same company. If your search for Herschel turned up backpacks, that's Herschel Supply Co., a separate business.

Hershel Sigma AI also has no connection to Sigma Dialer, SigmaMind AI or other companies with Sigma in the name. Searching the full name, Hershel Sigma AI, is the quickest way to land here.
