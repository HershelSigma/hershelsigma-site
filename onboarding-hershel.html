Welcome to Hershel: your next steps

For floors on a Hershel dialer · Updated October 6, 2026

Hershel Sigma stands up a dialer server for your floor alone and runs it for you, 24/7. Here is what to have ready, and the steps from signing to your first live day.

Have these ready

  • Managers. Name and email for each person who runs the floor. Each gets an email invite to the dashboard; read-only viewer seats are available too.
  • Reps. Full name and email for each rep (mobile optional). The email is their Google sign-in on your rep console.
  • Campaigns, lists and statuses. What you dial today, so it carries over with the same names and dispositions.
  • Carrier access. Your carrier account and caller-ID numbers. Twilio: the Account SID, plus a Standard API key (its SID starts with SK) and its secret. Telnyx: an API key and the SIP Connection ID.
  • GoHighLevel, if Closable is on your plan. A Private Integration Token (sub-account Settings > Private Integrations) and the Location ID (Settings > Business Profile).
  • Hours and alerts. Your timezone, your calling hours, and a mobile number for critical alerts.

Your next steps

  1. HershelWe stand up your dialer. A dedicated server for your floor alone, connected to your dashboard, with your campaigns, lists and statuses carried over.
  2. HershelWe connect your carrier and caller IDs. The Shield starts watching every caller ID.
  3. YouAccept your invite. Open the email invite within 7 days, choose your name and password, and you land in setup.
  4. YouFinish setup. At dashboard.hershelsigma.ai the setup wizard asks only for what is still missing: your alert phone, any keys not yet connected (it lists Closable as Talky-Sigma), and your team: managers, read-only viewers, and agents who get SMS alerts only. Then press Save & Deploy.
  5. YouAdd your reps. Team > Reps > Add rep. Each login copies your template rep (same group, campaigns and inbound groups) with a phone line of its own. The password shows once: copy it and hand it to the rep. Reps sign in at your Rep console, linked in the dashboard sidebar.
  6. TogetherGo live. Dialler Manager (AI Manager in your dashboard) takes over dial pace under the drop-rate ceiling you choose, and Closable starts briefing appointment-set calls into GoHighLevel. The Setup card on your dashboard tracks four checks (Dialer connected, Services configured, Manager joined, Services checked in) and goes away once all are done.

Day to day, your sidebar has Command (your assistant), Dashboard, Campaigns, Lists, Reports, Recordings, AI Manager, The Shield, Team and Settings. Change your timezone and hours anytime under Settings > Organization; services sleep outside them.

Questions at any step? Email info@hershelsigma.ai.
