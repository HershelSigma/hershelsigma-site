What the HighLevel app does

The Hershel app links one HighLevel sub-account to your Hershel organization. Once it is connected:

  • Calls land on your contacts. With Send calls to HighLevel switched on, each call a rep handles goes on the contact with that number: a note with the outcome, rep, call length, call time, the rep's notes and a recording link, plus a tag like hershel: busy you can use in workflows. If no contact has the number, Hershel creates one.
  • Send to Hershel workflow step. Add it to a HighLevel workflow and give it a Hershel list ID. Each contact that reaches the step is added to that list on your dialer.
  • Hershel in the left menu. Reps open the Hershel page inside the sub-account and land on their rep console.

Connect a sub-account

  1. Sign in to the Hershel dashboard as an admin or manager.
  2. Go to Settings, find the GoHighLevel card under Integrations, and click Connect HighLevel.
  3. In HighLevel, pick the sub-account to connect and approve the access the app asks for.
  4. HighLevel sends you back to Hershel Settings, where the card shows the connected sub-account.
  5. Switch on Send calls to HighLevel if you want call notes and tags on your contacts.

The link from the Connect HighLevel button works for 15 minutes. If it runs out, click the button again.

Disconnect or uninstall

In Hershel Settings, on the GoHighLevel card under Integrations, click Disconnect and confirm. Hershel deletes its stored access to the sub-account and stops writing to it. You can also uninstall the app from the sub-account in HighLevel. To have the rest of your HighLevel-related data deleted from Hershel, email us; the privacy policy lists what that covers.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Hershel account to use the HighLevel app?

Yes. The app connects a HighLevel sub-account to a Hershel organization, so the dialing happens in Hershel. A manager or admin in Hershel starts the connection from Settings.

Will Hershel change or delete my existing contacts?

Hershel adds to contacts. It posts a note and a tag for each call, and creates a contact only when no contact in the sub-account has the number that was called. It does not delete contacts, notes or tags.

Are test calls sent to HighLevel?

No. Calls on campaigns or by users marked as test in Hershel are left out.

Who can open the recording link on a call note?

Anyone who has the link. The link stops working once the sub-account is disconnected from Hershel or the campaign is no longer the organization's.
